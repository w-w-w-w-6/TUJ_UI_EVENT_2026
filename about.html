About HackathonCorp

We are a company made of passionate programmers base in Japan who want to show people who are new to hackathon that there is nothing to fear. All professional were once beginners, and we are here to help speed up this process.

We provide accurate test to match you to hackathons at your level and personalized courses to prepare you for those hackathons. We also connect beginners with experienced programmers.
